14. PLANNING RULES
==================================================

1. Hard chapters should receive priority and longer focused sessions.
2. Medium chapters should receive regular, shorter sessions.
3. Easy chapters should receive quick revision and question practice.
4. Rotate subjects instead of completing one entire subject before moving to another.
5. Include school assignments, worksheets and tests in the schedule.
6. Keep spaced revision reviews separate from first-time revision.
7. Do not automatically mark uncompleted tasks as missed.
8. Adjust estimated times based on actual performance and test results.
